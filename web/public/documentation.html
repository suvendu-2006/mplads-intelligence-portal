01 / The Challenge

The MPLADS Problem in Plain English

Every Member of Parliament is allocated ₹5 Crores annually for local infrastructure like schools, piped water, roads, and clinics. However, manual paper file audits cannot keep up with the massive volume of projects.

👻

1. Ghost Works

Civil projects marked as "Completed" on paper where either zero physical construction took place on site, or where zero funds were actually disbursed through bank accounts to contractors.

Solution: Dual-Ledger Triangulation (e-Sakshi + PFMS)
✂️

2. Artificial Bill-Splitting

Contractors divide a ₹20 Lakh project into multiple smaller ₹4.9 Lakh sub-orders to deliberately evade statutory public e-tendering rules required under General Financial Rules (GFR Rule 157).

Solution: ₹4.5L–₹4.99L Window Clustering Detection
📈

3. CPWD Price Inflation

Local implementing agencies bill works at 200% to 300% over the official CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates (DSR), siphoning public treasury funds through unverified item markups.

Solution: Automated DSR 2023 Rate Verification
⏳

4. Auditor Fatigue & File Backlog

With over 127,000+ active works nationwide, vigilance officers are overwhelmed with paper files. Random audits check less than 2% of projects, letting fraud go undetected.

Solution: Capacity-Aware Top 1% Triage Queues
02 / How It Works

End-to-End 5-Stage Forensic Workflow

SATARK-MPLADS processes data in an automated, five-stage pipeline from raw government ingestion to court-ready evidence dossiers.

STAGE 1

Multi-Source Ingestion & Data Lake

Simultaneously ingests physical work records from MoSPI e-SAKSHI (53,197 works) and central banking disbursement ledgers from PFMS (29,000 bank vouchers). Ingests official CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates (DSR 2023) and DataMeet 543 constituency GIS boundaries. Data contracts are strictly enforced via Pandera schema validation.

STAGE 2

Vectorized Feature Extraction & NLP Embeddings

Calculates expenditure velocity, time delays between sanctions and completion, and voucher digit distributions. Uses Sentence-BERT transformer embeddings (384-dimensional dense vectors) to convert Bill of Quantity (BoQ) work descriptions into mathematical representations.

STAGE 3

15-Detector Algorithmic Screening Core

Runs 15 specialized mathematical and statutory anomaly detectors in parallel. Evaluates cost inflation, text cloning, delay violations, and Benford's Law Chi-squared statistics to calculate a composite monotonic risk score (0.0 to 1.0) with zero black-box bias.

STAGE 4

Capacity-Aware 3-Tier Audit Triage Queues

Instead of creating thousands of spam alerts, the system automatically isolates the top 1% highest-risk projects into an immediate on-site inspection queue, top 5% into a desk review queue, and fast-tracks the clean 74% of projects for swift fund disbursement.

STAGE 5

Interactive Forensic Console & SHA-256 Audit Trail

District Collectors and auditors access complete evidence dossiers with one click. When an inspector visits the site and commits a verdict (CONFIRMED_FRAUD or CLEARED), the finding is permanently sealed with an immutable SHA-256 cryptographic hash for legal non-repudiation.

04 / Operational Triage

Capacity-Aware Triage: Eliminating Alert Fatigue

A system that generates 10,000 alerts is useless because no district collector has the staff to read them. SATARK automatically triages cases based on district administrative inspection capacity:

TIER 1 (Top 1%)

Critical Vigilance

Immediate physical on-site inspection by DM flying squad. Asset impoundment, contractor freeze, and statutory show-cause notice.

TIER 2 (Top 5%)

Priority Desk Review

Detailed cross-ledger verification of Measurement Books (MB), procurement receipts, and contractor bank history.

TIER 3 (Top 20%)

Routine Monitoring

Sample statistical audits and automated milestone tracking alerts with no immediate on-site dispatch.

CLEAN (74%)

Fast-Track Release

Verified legitimate projects. Automatically fast-tracked for prompt tranche releases without bureaucratic bottlenecks.

05 / Security & Feasibility

Built for Real-World District Administration

Zero Hardware Footprint

Runs entirely inside standard web browsers on existing office PCs and smartphones in district magistrate offices. Zero new computers or specialized on-premise servers required.

Zero Software Licensing Fees

Built 100% on open-source technologies (Python FastAPI, React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Leaflet GIS). Zero recurring per-user seat licenses or vendor lock-in.

Site inspection photos, GPS coordinates, and inspector conclusions are sealed with SHA-256 cryptographic hashes. Records cannot be altered, deleted, or back-dated post-facto.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Tailored consoles for MoSPI Apex Authorities, State Nodal Officers, District Magistrates, Members of Parliament, and Public Citizens.

07 / Evaluation Readiness

Frequently Asked Questions by Evaluators

Q: Can this be piloted immediately with zero government downtime?
Yes. SATARK-MPLADS is read-only non-intrusive. It connects to existing CSV/API data exports from e-SAKSHI and PFMS without modifying the existing databases or requiring procedural changes for district staff.
Q: What if a newly elected MP has only sanctioned 5 works? Will the AI falsely flag them?
No. Detector D13 applies Empirical Bayes (James-Stein) shrinkage to shrink small-sample variance toward the national prior. Low-volume representatives are statistically protected from false-positive skew.
Q: How do we prevent auditors or politicians from deleting audit records?
All ground inspection dossiers, photos, and verdicts are stamped with SHA-256 cryptographic hashes in an append-only ledger. Records cannot be edited, back-dated, or deleted post-facto.
Q: Where can we test the live application right now?
The portal is deployed live at https://mplads-intelligence-portal.vercel.app and the full open-source codebase is public at GitHub.
