The Real-World Problem Explained Simply

Under the MPLADS scheme, the Central Government allocates ₹5 Crores annually to each Member of Parliament (totaling over ₹4,000 Crores nationwide per year) to build essential community infrastructure: rural drinking water plants, schools, solar lights, and paved roads.

However, the Comptroller & Auditor General of India (CAG) reports and ground inspections repeatedly reveal that manual auditing across 127,000+ active works cannot catch sophisticated leaks:

❌ Ghost Works

Projects officially marked "Completed" in municipal registers, but zero money was paid, or the asset physically does not exist on the ground.

❌ Bill-Splitting (< ₹5L)

Contractors split a ₹20 Lakh project into multiple ₹4.9 Lakh chunks to bypass mandatory public e-tenders required under GFR Rule 157.

❌ CPWD Rate Overruns

Contractors bill cement, bricks, and labor at 200% to 300% over the official CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates (DSR), siphoning public money.

❌ Manual Audit Backlog

District Magistrates and vigilance squads are overwhelmed with physical paper Measurement Books (MB) and cannot inspect even 2% of works.

How SATARK-MPLADS Works (The 5 Stages)

Instead of a black-box AI that gives unexplainable scores, SATARK uses a deterministic, rule-based and natural language forensic architecture structured into five clear stages:

Stage 1

Dual-Ledger Data Lake Ingestion

Collects physical project records from MoSPI e-SAKSHI (53,197 works) and reconciles them with official PFMS central bank disbursement vouchers (29,000 vouchers). Validated by Pandera schema data contracts.

Stage 2

Feature Engineering & Semantic NLP (Sentence-BERT)

Converts work descriptions into mathematical embeddings using Sentence-BERT to detect renamed cloned projects across years. Computes sanction-to-completion time deltas and voucher digit distributions.

Stage 3

15-Detector Forensic Screening Core

Runs 15 mathematical and regulatory rules in parallel (evaluating CPWD benchmark rates, Benford's Law Chi-squared tests, and GFR tendering thresholds) to calculate a composite risk score (0.0 to 1.0).

Stage 4

Capacity-Aware 3-Tier Triage Queues

Eliminates alert fatigue. Automatically isolates the Top 1% Critical Projects (Tier 1) for immediate DM on-site inspection, Top 5% (Tier 2) for desk review, and clears clean works for instant fund release.

Stage 5

Interactive Console & Cryptographic Audit Vault

Field officers record ground-truth findings (CONFIRMED_FRAUD or CLEARED) with photos and GPS stamps. Every record is sealed with an immutable SHA-256 hash for court-ready legal non-repudiation.

Feasibility, Deployment & Security

💻 Zero Hardware Footprint

Runs directly inside standard web browsers on existing NIC office desktop computers and smartphones. No local database installation or servers needed at district offices.

💸 Zero Software License Fees

Engineered 100% on open-source technologies (Python FastAPI, React, TypeScript, Leaflet GIS). Zero recurring per-user seat fees or commercial subscriptions.

🔒 Tamper-Evident SHA-256 Vault

Inspection photos and audit logs are cryptographically sealed with SHA-256 digital stamps. Records cannot be edited or deleted after submission, guaranteeing court-ready evidence.

👥 Role-Based Access Control

Dedicated customized consoles for MoSPI Apex Authorities, State Nodal Officers, District Magistrates, Members of Parliament, and Public Citizens.
