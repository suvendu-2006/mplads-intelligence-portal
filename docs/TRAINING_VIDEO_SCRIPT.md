# MPLADS Platform Training Video Scripts

## Module 1: System Onboarding & Navigation (30 Minutes)
* **Audience**: All authorized users (Viewers, Analysts, Auditors, Admins)
* **Key Learning Objectives**:
  1. Secure authentication and session security.
  2. Navigating the Executive Dashboard and understanding portfolio health indicators.
  3. Correctly interpreting evidence signals vs. legal fraud accusations.
  4. Filtering projects by district (IDA), MP portfolio, and detector categories.

---

## Module 2: Field Auditor Inspection Protocol (2 Hours)
* **Audience**: Field Auditors, Executive Engineers, and Quality Control Officers
* **Key Learning Objectives**:
  1. Operating the 1,000-work stratified audit sample.
  2. Minimum physical verification requirements:
     - Geotagged photographic capture of the asset.
     - Measurement Book (Form 23) cross-verification against sanctioned BOQ.
     - Verification of completion certificate and utilization statements.
  3. Ground-truth label recording protocol in the Field Audit Desk.
  4. Dual-reviewer adjudication and inter-rater agreement standards.

---

## Module 3: System Administration & Operational Governance (1 Hour)
* **Audience**: System Administrators & DevOps Teams
* **Key Learning Objectives**:
  1. User provisioning, role assignment, and access revocation.
  2. Inspecting the immutable `audit_logs` trail for administrative accountability.
  3. Database backup verification, migration management with Alembic, and emergency rollback procedures.
  4. Monitoring data freshness and Population Stability Index (PSI) drift alerts.
